OPERATOR PRECEDENCE AND ASSOCIATIVITY

Operator       Precedence       Associativity
------------------------------------------------
()             Highest          N/A
^              3                Right to left
*, /, %        2                Left to right
, -            1                Left to right

Parentheses are solved first. Exponentiation comes next. Multiplication,
division and modulus have the same precedence. Addition and subtraction
have the lowest precedence in this table.
