Infix:
A - B * C + D ^ E / F

Prefix:
+ - A * B C / ^ D E F

Method:
1. Infix expression reverse ki jati hai.
2. Parentheses swap kiye jate hain.
3. Precedence aur associativity stack mein check hoti hai.
4. Reversed postfix ko reverse karke prefix result milta hai.

Stack conversion trace:

Original infix: A - B * C + D ^ E / F
Reversed and brackets swapped: F / E ^ D + C * B - A
Reversed postfix: FE/D^CB*A-+
